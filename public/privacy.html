CloseAssist™ Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

CloseAssist ("CloseAssist," "we," "us") is a web application for licensed real estate agents, operated by Miguel Soza, a licensed Illinois real estate broker with Smart Home Realty. This policy explains what information CloseAssist handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions: msozarealestate@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Most of what you enter into CloseAssist — your brand profile, saved drafts, CRM contacts, notes, and tasks — is stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it. You can export your CRM data as a CSV file at any time from within the app.

2. Information processed to provide AI features

When you use CloseAssist's AI drafting and analysis tools, the text you submit is sent through our server functions to an AI model provider (currently Anthropic) solely to generate the response you requested. We do not sell this content, use it for advertising, or use it to train models. Requests are rate-limited and logged in aggregate for abuse prevention.

3. Connected third-party services

CloseAssist lets you connect services you already use, always under your own account and only with your authorization:

  • Follow Up Boss — if you connect it, CloseAssist uses the API key you provide to read and draft against your own account.
  • dotloop — if you connect it, you sign in with your own dotloop account and authorize CloseAssist via OAuth 2.0. CloseAssist stores an access/refresh token server-side, encrypted, solely to perform the actions you request (such as creating loops, uploading documents you choose, and showing loop status). Your forms, documents, and signatures remain in your dotloop account, governed by dotloop's own terms and privacy notice. CloseAssist does not provide, host, or redistribute real estate forms.
  • MLS/IDX data — listing data displayed in CloseAssist is provided under licence from the applicable MLS and is shown subject to that MLS's rules.

You can disconnect any connected service at any time; disconnecting revokes CloseAssist's access going forward.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share your CRM contacts, client information, or documents with other CloseAssist users or with advertisers.
  • We do not access a connected account except to perform actions you initiate in the app.

5. Security

Connection tokens for third-party services are stored server-side and encrypted at rest, and are never exposed to other users. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the data involved.

6. Your choices and rights

Because most CloseAssist data lives on your own device or in your own connected accounts, you control it directly: edit or delete it in the app, export it, or disconnect a service to cut off access. If you believe we hold any personal information about you on our servers and want it deleted, contact us at the email above and we will comply promptly.

7. Children

CloseAssist is a professional tool for licensed real estate agents and is not directed to children under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the effective date above.
